Who We Are?

Habesha Market is your local digital and physical marketplace in Ethiopia, connecting people with authentic local goods, fresh spices, and traditional crafts. We celebrate our rich culture by bringing the best local makers and buyers together in one easy place.

  • Proudly based in Ethiopia, supporting local farmers, weavers, and small business owners.
  • Focused on pure, natural, and traditional goods passed down through generations.
  • A friendly and open space designed to make shopping simple and safe for everyone.

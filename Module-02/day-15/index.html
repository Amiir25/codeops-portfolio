Our Story

Habesha Eatery started in 2015 as a small family kitchen in the Bole neighborhood of Addis Ababa. What began as a place for neighbors to share coffee and injera has grown into a home for anyone who wants to experience real Ethiopian hospitality. Every dish on our menu is inspired by recipes passed down from our grandmothers, cooked slowly and shared the traditional way — on one big platter, with your hands, with your family.
